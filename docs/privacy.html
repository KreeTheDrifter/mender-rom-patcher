Mender Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Mender is an Android app that applies IPS, BPS and UPS patch files to files you choose. This policy explains what information is involved when you use it.

Your files stay on your device

Mender reads the files you pick with Android's file picker and writes the patched file where you choose to save it. All patching happens on your device. Your files and their contents are never uploaded, collected or shared, and Mender doesn't access any other files.

Advertising

The free version of Mender shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), Mender asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from Privacy choices in Mender's menu. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.

Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. The developer receives no payment details. Mender only asks Google Play whether your account owns the purchase so it can turn ads off, and remembers the answer on your device.

What the developer collects

The developer of Mender doesn't collect, store or sell any personal information. Mender has no accounts, no analytics and no servers of its own.

Children

Mender isn't directed at children under 13, and ads are not personalized for users identified as children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer email address shown on Mender's Google Play page.
